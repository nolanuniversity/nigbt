Scholarships & Financial Aid

Making capability-building education accessible to talented professionals and entrepreneurs worldwide

Financial Support

Available Scholarships

Nolan Institute offers a range of scholarships to support exceptional learners across Africa and beyond

Founding Cohort Scholarship

Full Tuition

Awarded to a limited number of exceptional applicants joining the Founding Cohort 2026, recognising outstanding potential in finance, technology or entrepreneurship.

  • Full tuition coverage for one programme
  • Direct mentorship from Nolan Institute faculty
  • Guaranteed applied project placement
Deadline: Priority — Dec 1, 2025

Entrepreneurship & Innovation Scholarship

Up to Full Tuition

Designed for founders and enterprise builders who are actively working on a venture or innovation project.

  • Full or partial tuition
  • Access to venture mentorship
  • Networking with investors and industry leaders
Deadline: Rolling

Merit Scholarship

Up to 50% Tuition

Recognising academic excellence and demonstrated achievement in finance, technology, business or entrepreneurship.

  • 25% to 50% tuition reduction
  • Automatic consideration with application
  • Renewable based on programme performance
Deadline: Priority — Dec 1, 2025

Women in Finance & Technology Scholarship

$25,000

Supporting women pursuing careers in finance, capital markets, technology and digital leadership.

  • $25,000 award
  • Mentorship from women leaders in finance and tech
  • Access to the Women in Finance & Tech network
Deadline: Mar 1, 2025

Global Leaders Scholarship

$20,000

For international students who demonstrate leadership potential and a commitment to building capability in their home countries.

  • $20,000 award
  • International student support services
  • Leadership development programme
Deadline: Rolling

Corporate & Workforce Access Grant

Variable

Providing financial support to professionals and teams with demonstrated economic need, including sponsored workforce development candidates.

  • Award amounts vary based on need
  • Supporting documentation required
  • Available for individual and corporate applicants
Deadline: Rolling

How to Apply for Scholarships

Most scholarships are awarded through the Central Application. Simply indicate your interest in the application form. For need-based grants, please submit supporting documentation along with your application.
