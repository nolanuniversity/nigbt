Questions

Pricing FAQ

Common questions about fees, payment and international pricing.

Why do specialist subjects cost more than standard subjects?

Specialist subjects — such as Financial Modelling, Investment & Capital Markets, Data Analytics and AI — require more technical instruction, software and tools, modelling exercises, datasets and specialist faculty. Standard subjects focus on management, leadership and business capability. The pricing reflects the difference in delivery cost and technical depth.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. Every Professional Programme and Advanced Programme includes a structured payment plan — a registration amount upfront followed by instalments. Click "View Payment Plan" on any longer programme to see the exact breakdown in ZMW and USD.

Why do you show two currencies?

We charge the same fee to every student — no country-by-country tuition structures. To make the fee meaningful for both local and international students, every price is shown in Zambian Kwacha (ZMW) alongside its USD equivalent. The USD figure is a reference conversion at 1 USD ≈ 20 ZMW. Your final invoice will be issued in the currency most appropriate for your location.

What payment methods are accepted?

Mobile money (Airtel, MTN, Zamtel), card, bank transfer, and international payments. If you're outside Zambia, USD card or international transfer options are available.

Is the application fee separate from programme fees?

Yes. There is a separate non-refundable application fee — ZK250 (≈ US$12.50) for Zambian and African applicants, or US$25 (≈ K500) for international applicants. This is paid when you submit your application, before programme fees.

What happens if I need to defer or withdraw?

Payment terms, deferral and withdrawal conditions are set out in your programme agreement. Please contact admissions before making a decision — our team will walk you through the options.
