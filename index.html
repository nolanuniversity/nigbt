Who We Are

About Nolan Institute

Learn. Innovate. Lead.

Nolan Institute develops people, businesses and organisations through technology-enabled education in Business, Finance, Technology and Entrepreneurship.

Our programmes are practical, applied and designed around the real capability gaps that individuals and organisations face — from financial modelling and capital markets, to data analytics, AI and digital transformation.

Our Mission

To develop the financial, technological, business and entrepreneurial capabilities that individuals and organisations need to thrive in a changing world.
